Reason for rerun
----------------
The previous PCN MBB result around CD-L1 x1000 ~= 7.43 was discarded
after the PCN ablation protocol was rechecked while synchronizing the
PCN and ShapeNet-55 ablation tables.

Final canonical PCN training
----------------------------

scripts/Ablation/PCN_ablation/train_pcn_ours_clean.py

scripts/Ablation/PCN_ablation/run_ablation.py --mode ours

Canonical MBB forward:
G2S:
    sem_feat = sem_attn(
        sem_token,
        geo_tokens.detach()
    )

S2G:
    geo_feat = geo_attn(
        geo_tokens,
        sem_token
    )

The two directions are computed in parallel from the original tokens.
Both residual gates and LayerNorm outputs are retained.
